# M7 Execution Blocker

Comparative execution is prohibited. M7 corrects and regression-tests three implementation defects discovered before outcome analysis, but the non-M role action surface remains incompletely formalized for production execution.

The v0.4 authority names the affected S1/S2 families and qualitative transformations. It does not fully freeze every quantity, trigger, completion condition, or role-controller decision rule needed to make those families executable without adding new benchmark design. Those choices must be closed prospectively and audited before a new execution lock is issued.

No M6 comparative outcomes may be used to choose those closures.
